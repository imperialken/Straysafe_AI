The problem

A blind spot in everyday
urban navigation.

Stray dog encounters can create real safety concerns for pedestrians and bikers. The project identifies a missing layer: real-time information about stray dog zones.

Instead of waiting until an encounter happens, StraySafe AI aims to make risk visible before the user reaches it.

01

Unpredictable encounters

Users may have little warning before entering a high-risk area.

02

No live safety layer

Conventional navigation does not account for stray dog zones.

03

Route decisions happen late

Awareness needs to arrive early enough to change the path.
